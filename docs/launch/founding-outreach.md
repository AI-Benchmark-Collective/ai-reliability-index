# Founding-contributor outreach

## Qualification rule

Contact someone only when you can cite a specific project, paper, course, repository,
or domain experience connected to one open responsibility. Every invitation links to
one issue with a bounded deliverable.

## Initial message

> Hi [Name] — I’m assembling the first 15 contributors to AI Benchmark Collective’s
> AI Reliability Index. We’re testing whether agent leaderboard scores survive repeated
> real-world runs, with complete traces, cost reporting, and independent review.
>
> I found your work on [specific work]. Would you be willing to own or review
> [specific issue + link]? The initial scope is approximately [honest estimate].
> Accepted work receives permanent release credit and eligibility for founding
> membership. No promotional posting is required.

## Follow-up after five days

> Just resurfacing this once because [specific reason their expertise fits]. If the
> timing is wrong, no response is needed. If useful, I can narrow the contribution to
> [smaller concrete review].

Do not send a third unsolicited message.

## Daily operating target

- Research and contact 10 qualified people.
- Personalize every invitation.
- Record contact, relevant work, requested issue, date, response, and next action.
- Reply to interested contributors within 24 hours.
- Never imply that membership, authorship, or rankings can be purchased.
