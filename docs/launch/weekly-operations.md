# Weekly community and distribution operations

## Cadence

- **Monday:** Publish one bounded contributor task and review stale ownership.
- **Wednesday:** Publish one technical finding, failure case, or methodology decision.
- **Friday:** Release one runnable demo, reproduction, or benchmark result.
- **Sunday:** Credit one contributor and publish the next-week roadmap.

## Service levels

- Acknowledge credible issues and pull requests within 24 hours.
- Give an actionable first review within 72 hours.
- If work is out of scope, explain why and identify the next review window.
- Never leave a claimed issue without a maintainer response for seven days.

## Funnel metrics

Record weekly:

- unique repository visitors and referring sites;
- clones and successful local runs;
- discussion participants;
- first-time pull requests;
- accepted outside contributions;
- active contributors and public members;
- independent reproductions;
- report downloads and external citations; and
- median time to first maintainer response.

Stars are a reach signal, not proof of usage or reliability.
