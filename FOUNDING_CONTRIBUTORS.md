# Founding Contributor Charter

The inaugural cohort is limited to 15 people because every accepted contribution must
receive substantive review and clear ownership.

## Open responsibilities

| Seats | Responsibility | Evidence expected |
|---:|---|---|
| 3 | Evaluation engineering | Runner, adapter, schema, or evaluator work |
| 3 | Benchmark-task design | Accepted task plus reproducibility evidence |
| 2 | Statistical review | Review or implementation of reported measures |
| 2 | Safety and failure taxonomy | Operational definitions tested on sample traces |
| 2 | Independent reproduction | Recreated environment and verified results |
| 1 | Visualization | Accessible, accurate result communication |
| 1 | Technical writing | Release methodology or limitations section |
| 1 | Community operations | Contributor intake and response system |

## Recognition

Founding Contributors receive permanent credit in the inaugural release and a link to
the accepted work supporting that designation. Authorship follows substantive scholarly
contribution; it is not promised in exchange for promotion or nominal affiliation.

## Admission

1. Claim or propose a bounded issue.
2. Complete an accepted contribution or independent review.
3. Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
4. Receive maintainer review under `docs/governance.md`.
5. Make organization membership public only if the contributor wishes.

The roster will be added here as contributions are accepted. Empty honorary seats and
pay-to-join membership are prohibited.
