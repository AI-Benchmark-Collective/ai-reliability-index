# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a rigorous, welcoming community in which people can contribute regardless of background, identity, experience level, or institutional affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, methods, code, and evidence—not people.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Give actionable, specific review.
- Respect privacy, licenses, embargoes, and responsible disclosure.
- Credit contributors and sources accurately.
- Accept correction when evidence changes.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discrimination, personal attacks, and deceptive participation.

## Research integrity

Fabricating runs, hiding material configuration changes, manipulating denominators, suppressing unfavorable results, misrepresenting authorship, or submitting data without authorization is unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in organization repositories, Discussions, issues, pull requests, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to an organization owner or repository maintainer. Do not place sensitive allegations or personal information in public issues.

Maintainers will assess available evidence, protect confidentiality where possible, recuse themselves when conflicted, and apply proportionate measures ranging from a private clarification through temporary restrictions or removal. Immediate restrictions may be used to protect people, systems, or evidence while a matter is reviewed.

Retaliation against good-faith reporters or review participants is prohibited.
